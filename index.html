Training samples by Feralucce Savage: three film-production samples (a branching scenario, a call-sheet course and a set safety orientation with its evaluation plan), a corporate course built in Articulate Rise 360, and a three-course training suite for the 20 Below tabletop RPG, built to its brand. For new team leads and trainers asked to "fix" mistakes on the job: when a job aid beats a training session, how to choose the format, five design rules, and a 30-minute field test. Follows a fictional warehouse supervisor through a real revision.

Film production

Your First Day as a PA

Branching scenario · about 15 minutes · Savage Light Studios style

A production assistant's first shooting day on an indie feature: call time, the radio, holding a lockup out of frame and out of the eyelines, chain of command, calling a safety hold, and wrap. Choices move three meters, two branches recover from mistakes, and set maps show where to stand.
